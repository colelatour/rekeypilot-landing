AI reads. Rules verify. You approve.

Emails are messy, so we use AI to read them. Orders and invoices are not, so we don't let AI create them on its own.

What the AI does

Reads the email body, PDF, or scan and pulls out what's being ordered, by whom, and how it should ship. That's it. It interprets; it doesn't decide.

What the checks do

Every interpreted field is checked against your actual records: customer list, SKU catalog, price list, ship-to addresses, and the rules you set for specific customers. A match either passes or gets flagged.

What you do

Look at what's flagged, fix it if needed, and approve. Only then does anything get created in ShipStation or QuickBooks.

RekeyPilot never creates an order or an invoice without a person approving it first.

Questions

Does it create orders automatically?

No. RekeyPilot prepares the ShipStation order and QuickBooks invoice and holds them until someone on your team approves. We'll consider optional auto-approval for specific trusted customers later, and only after a team has seen it be right for a while.

What if customers use their own product names?

That's the normal case. RekeyPilot matches customer terminology to your SKUs, and when it isn't sure it shows you the candidates instead of guessing. Once you pick, it remembers that mapping for that customer.

Does it handle PDFs and scans, or just email text?

Both. Emailed text, attached PDFs, and scanned or photographed forms. The messier the input, the more likely something gets flagged for review rather than passed silently.

Does it change anything in my existing QuickBooks or ShipStation data?

It reads your customer, item, and price records so it can match against them. It only writes new orders and invoices, and only after approval. It doesn't edit existing records.

What does early access mean?

RekeyPilot is being built with a small number of wholesale businesses. Sending a sample PO gets you a hand-prepared example of the output and a conversation about whether your workflow is a fit. There's no product to install yet.

What does it cost?

We're working out pricing with early customers. Expect a monthly subscription based on order volume, well below enterprise automation tools. We'd rather tell you a real number after we've seen your POs than a made-up one now.
